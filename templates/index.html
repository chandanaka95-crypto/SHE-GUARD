"The 3-Second Scanning Habit"

Whenever you step out of a vehicle, enter an ATM vestibule, or walk onto a quiet street, pause for 3 seconds. Look left, right, and behind. Keep your chin up and both ears unobstructed. Attackers target people who appear distracted by their smartphones.
