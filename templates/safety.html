Travelling Alone Precautions

SOLO TRANSIT PROTOCOL
  • Always Share Live GPS: Before stepping out late, share your real-time WhatsApp or Google Maps location with a family member.
  • Keep One Ear Free: Avoid wearing noise-canceling headphones in both ears so you remain conscious of footsteps and approaching engines.
  • Hotel Check-in Discretion: If the front desk clerk says your room number aloud, politely ask for a different room to preserve privacy.
  • Walk With Purpose: Head high, shoulders back, eyes alert. Confident body language deters opportunist offenders.

Public Transport & Cabs

CAB & BUS SAFETY
  • Match License Plates: Always match the driver photo, vehicle model, and registration plate before opening the door.
  • Check Child Locks: Check that rear doors open from the inside before departing. Sit diagonally opposite the driver.
  • Simulate a Check-in Call: Say loudly on the phone: "Hey, I just boarded cab number DL-1Y-4560, reaching home in 20 minutes."
  • Refuse Off-Route Turns: If a driver deviates from the GPS navigation, question it firmly immediately or demand to alight in a lit area.

Cyber Safety & Anti-Stalking

DIGITAL PRIVACY
  • Delayed Posting: Do not post social media stories with real-time location check-ins until after you have left the venue.
  • Audit Device Permissions: Regularly inspect Location and Microphone permissions granted to third-party apps on your phone.
  • Preserve Digital Evidence: If receiving stalker messages, never delete them. Take full screenshots showing numbers and timestamps.
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Use Authenticator apps (Google/Microsoft Authenticator) instead of SMS OTPs for email and social accounts.

Legal Rights & Protections

YOUR LEGAL POWER
  • Zero FIR Right: A woman can file a First Information Report (FIR) at ANY police station regardless of where the incident occurred.
  • Arrest Protections: According to the law, a woman cannot be arrested between sunset and sunrise except under extraordinary magistrates' orders.
  • Right to Confidentiality: Sexual harassment or abuse victims have the right to record their statements in private before a female officer or magistrate.
  • PoSH Act Protection: The Prevention of Sexual Harassment Act mandates an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) in every organization with 10+ employees.
